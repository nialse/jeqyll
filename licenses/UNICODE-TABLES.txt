Unicode 16.0.0 property and case-fold data

The numeric ranges, property aliases and case-fold mappings in
src/regex_unicode_data.ll are data-only transcriptions of
vendor/oniguruma/src/unicode_property_data.c and unicode_fold_data.c in the
jq source pinned by PATHS.md. No lookup or matching implementation from
those files is included.
